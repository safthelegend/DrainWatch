    How it works

    Public data in, a plain-language risk level out

    Every 15 minutes DrainWatch reads tide gauges, forecasts, weather alerts and 311 reports from free government and open-data sources. No API keys, no tracking. For each hazard, a short, tested set of rules scores how bad conditions will get and scales it by how exposed each neighborhood is.

    risk = hazard (0–4) × exposure + alerts
